CV Maker & PDF Merger

Before you print

    1. Layout and style

    Dark blue, navy or dark green look most formal.

    Pick the size that matches where you're applying.

    2. Personal details

    Which job are you applying for? Recruiters read this first.

    3. Profile summary

    Aim for 2 to 4 sentences: your role, your strongest skill, and one result. Avoid "hard-working" or "team player" on their own.

    4. Work experience

    For each job, ask yourself: what changed because I was there? Use numbers where you can (percentages, counts, money, time saved). Start with a strong verb such as "Managed", "Reduced", "Built" or "Trained".

    5. Education

    List your most recent or most relevant qualification first. Leave out school results unless you're a school leaver.

    6. Skills

    Only list skills you could talk about confidently in an interview. Match them to words in the job advert.

    7. Optional sections

    For languages, use a level such as native, fluent, B2 or basic.

    "Available on request" is standard. Only give contact details if you've asked those people first.

    In the print dialog, untick "Headers and footers" and choose "Save as PDF". Aim for one page for under 10 years' experience, and no more than two pages in any case.

    Use the arrows to set the order. Files are merged in the order shown. HEIC photos are converted to JPG automatically, which can take a few seconds for large photos.

    Image placement (applies to photos only)

    Photos use the paper size chosen in the CV tab (section 1). Leave the rotation fix ticked unless a photo shows up turned.
